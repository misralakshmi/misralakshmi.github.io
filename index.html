Lakshmi Misra. I empower people and businesses to innovate, adopt AI and turn ideas into results at scale.

Innovation & AI Adoption Leader · Director, The Garage India, Microsoft

I grew up in Banaras and studied engineering in Bengaluru. I began my career in Titan’s corporate innovation team, building early smartwatches, and later worked with AI researchers and startups at IIIT Hyderabad. Those years taught me to start with people, empower them, and help them build their ideas into real products. Wherever I have worked, I have created spaces and launched programs before anyone asked and stayed with them until they became mainstream: incubators at IIIT Hyderabad, and The Garage Noida and AI Unlocked at Microsoft. Today I lead The Garage India, Microsoft’s innovation hub across four sites for its ~23,000-person India Development Centre.

Empower people. Build the spaces. Grow the community. Innovation follows.

Titan2006 – 2010 · Bengaluru

After studying engineering in Bengaluru, my career began at Titan as an engineer on new products. Titan was at heart a manufacturing company, and innovation there was an enabler rather than the goal: it had to work for the people making and using the product. I built 50 working OLED smartwatch prototypes and learned what it takes to carry an idea from the CEO’s first yes through planning, budgeting, testing and market research.

Each project meant working with four or five vendors and partners, including suppliers in China, Taiwan and Singapore. I was also part of the team that won the Tata Innovation Award for an RF-driven and a solar-powered product.

NASSCOM Foundation2015 – 2016 · Hyderabad

After a master’s in sociology at the University of Hyderabad, I joined NASSCOM Foundation and brought about 50 NGOs into its BigTech Program, connecting them with technology support.

Around the same time I volunteered as an incubation associate with UnLtd Hyderabad, working with early-stage ventures.

IIIT Hyderabad2016 – 2020 · Hyderabad

At IIIT Hyderabad my job was to give founders’ passion somewhere to grow. I joined the Centre for Innovation and Entrepreneurship as an incubation orchestrator and was promoted each year, first to head incubation and community, then to head industry relationships.

Over four years I helped build four incubators from the ground up with about $1M in government funding, connected around 70 startups with investors and corporates, and secured a $15M grant from the Department of Science and Technology to set up a Technology Innovation Hub in Data Sciences. I also built 50+ partnerships a year, including for the Kohli Center on Intelligent Systems, India’s largest academic AI cluster, and became the institute’s voice for startups as we repositioned the centre around deep tech.

In 2018 I was selected for the U.S. State Department’s International Visitor Leadership Program, representing India.

British Deputy High Commission2020 – 2021 · Hyderabad

In August 2020, in the middle of the pandemic, I joined the British Deputy High Commission to lead the UK India Tech Hub’s work in the Hyderabad region.

I launched two national digital-skills programs for startups, women entrepreneurs and working professionals, built partnerships with tech companies, NGOs and government to reach underserved groups and startups in Tier 2 and 3 cities, and worked with five international Tech Hubs on shared priorities.

Microsoft · The Garage India2021 – now · Hyderabad and Noida

I joined The Garage in Hyderabad in 2021 as a senior program manager and site lead, and I have grown with it: Director for Hyderabad and Noida from 2024, and Director of The Garage India since July 2026.

My work is about turning employees’ ideas into real products. I have co-led India’s Global Hackathon since 2021; it has been Microsoft’s largest venue every year since 2023, India teams won the Grand Prize three years running, and in 2024 I presented India’s results to Satya Nadella in Redmond. Through Innovators’ Arena, 14 of 21 employee projects have made it into production.

When AI changed how we work, I built a community-learning program that has now upskilled 16,000+ colleagues. And in 2024 I opened The Garage Noida from scratch; today 3,000 employees use it to learn and build together.

Alongside this work, and while raising two teenagers, I completed an Executive MBA at IIM Lucknow in 2025, with a dissertation on how women leaders adapt their leadership when they move to a new organization.
