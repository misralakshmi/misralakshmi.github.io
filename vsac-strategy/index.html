Operating map

How CS × X works in practice

VSAC operates in three contexts: industrial transformation, the national agenda and societal challenges. Partners in each bring work that Ashoka's units take on across computing and the liberal arts, and a set of mechanisms turns the work into value for partners and for Ashoka.

How partners connect

VSAC Industry Advisory Council

An advisory council of 12 to 15 senior leaders that connects VSAC's teaching and research to industry, opens doors to partners and builds a lasting funding base. It complements the academic Board of Advisors.
